# Privacy and legal review brief (UK/Wales)

This is a briefing document, not legal advice or a legal conclusion. A qualified
UK adviser must identify the correct entities, facts and applicable law before
the gate can close.

## System facts to confirm

- Cian AI Ltd, deployment operators, AI providers, validators and language
  profile bodies: controller, joint-controller or processor roles
- Exact purposes and lawful basis for interaction text, session metadata,
  attestations, proofs, appeals, reviewer records and security logs
- Data flow locations, sub-processors and international transfers
- Retention/deletion periods, consent withdrawal, access, objection, correction
  and erasure handling
- Whether children are likely users and which higher-protection duties apply
- Whether language, identity or inferred information may reveal special-category
  data in context
- Whether reward/entitlement design triggers financial-services, consumer,
  advertising, tax, employment, equality or other regulatory obligations
- Ownership/licensing of language datasets, profiles, logos and contributor work

## Required evidence

- Data inventory and flow diagram
- Controller/processor analysis and contracts
- Data Protection Impact Assessment where required
- Privacy notice and consent/lawful-basis design
- Retention schedule and rights-request procedure
- Provider transfer and deletion assessment
- Written legal classification of the entitlement and public claims
- Review of accessibility, equality and Welsh/minority-language governance

The ICO requires data protection by design and default throughout the lifecycle,
including limiting personal information to what is necessary for each purpose.
The review should use the ICO AI guidance and current UK GDPR resources, which
reflect changes including the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
