# Community code of conduct

Cian welcomes respectful participation across languages, cultures, disciplines
and levels of technical experience.

Participants must:

- treat people with dignity and avoid harassment, discrimination and personal attacks;
- respect Welsh speakers and other minority-language communities as partners, not test data;
- distinguish evidence from opinion and uncertainty from fact;
- protect personal information, credentials and confidential vulnerability details;
- give constructive criticism and credit other people's work; and
- avoid extractive, deceptive or discriminatory uses of the protocol.

Unacceptable conduct may result in content removal or temporary or permanent
exclusion from project spaces. Report community-conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner through the contact route in the GitHub organisation. Use
`SECURITY.md` for security vulnerabilities.

Project maintainers will assess reports as consistently and confidentially as
practicable. This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, community
events and representations made on behalf of the project.
